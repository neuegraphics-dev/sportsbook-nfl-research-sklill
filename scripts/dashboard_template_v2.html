Bet card

Every pick with money on it, in one place. Best bets are worth playing at today's number. Spreads, totals and props follow, each in its own group. Picks at $0 sit in Watch because the number moved or they're waiting on news. Everything else is under Passed.

Wong teasers

A 6-point teaser leg qualifies when a favorite sits at exactly −7.5 or an underdog sits at +1.5 to +2.5, with the game total at 47 or under. Every qualifying leg is listed. This list is never capped.

The slate

Every run logs the market's line and the model's number. The chart starts at the opening line and adds a point each time the lines are checked. Higher on the chart means the home team is more favored. Games where the model and market sit furthest apart come first.

Follow the money

Tickets count bettors. Money counts dollars. When the dollars pile onto one side and the line moves the other way, the book is respecting someone other than the public. Each card ends with what that means for the bet.

Splits from cleatz.com. A game without a fresh split this cycle keeps its last captured numbers. Treat exact percentages as directional.

Situational systems

Historical spots that have beaten the spread. Every block is one system that fits this week's real situation. Taller stacks mean more systems agree on the same side.

Player props

Opening numbers, captured when the books first posted them. Each card shows the confidence score, every reason for and against, and how this player has done against today's line this season.

Hit rates

Every graded prop and every logged game, scored against the call we made before kickoff. Pushes are left out of every rate.

Line Watch is personal research. Every lean is a read with a confidence score, never a guaranteed outcome.
